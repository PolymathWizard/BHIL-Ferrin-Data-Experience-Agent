Drift Ops sound pack

Thirty cues for a shipboard AI, synthesized entirely from code. Everything sits in D major pentatonic and signs off with the Longwake call: A, D, E, A.

The session runs about 35 seconds and shows how the loops and cues hand off as an agent's status changes.

Rendered at 48 kHz, 16-bit mono. Serve this folder over HTTP (GitHub Pages works) so the page can load the audio files. Loops on this page play from WAV, since MP3 padding breaks seamless looping.
