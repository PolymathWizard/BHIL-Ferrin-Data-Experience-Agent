# Attribution

FERRIN is a fork of [ruvnet/ruPet](https://github.com/ruvnet/ruPet) by Reuven Cohen (ruvnet), an MIT-licensed desktop pet for the ChatGPT and Codex desktop pet system. The original MIT notice is kept in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

What this fork keeps from ruPet: the pet package format (`pet.json` plus one sprite atlas), the atlas geometry (1536 x 2288, 8 x 11 cells of 192 x 208), the row order and frame counts, and the folder and packaging conventions.

What is new: all FERRIN artwork (drawn in code from `tools/ferrin_spec.py`), the character, lore and dialogue, the Drift Ops commands, the preview console, the sound pack, the voice prototype and the 3D study. None of ruPet's artwork or branding is used.

The acoustic-trap physics in the 3D study draws on published research, credited on its Specifications page: Marzo et al., Nature Communications (2015); Smalley et al., Nature (2018); Marzo and Drinkwater, PNAS (2019); Hirayama et al., Nature (2019).

FERRIN is not an official OpenAI product and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or ruvnet.
